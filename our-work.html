What We Do

Our Work in Ondo City

Every programme we run is driven by one belief: that no child should go without the essentials of a dignified life.

Our Mission

Play for Their World — With Us

God Made Mum Ondo was born from a single moment of heartbreak and a lifetime of determination. We exist to serve the most vulnerable children and families in Ondo City, Nigeria — providing what every human being deserves from birth: safety, nourishment, and opportunity.

From our holiday outreach drives to ongoing education support, each programme is designed with care, compassion, and community at its heart. We don't just deliver aid — we build relationships and restore hope.

Programmes

How We Make a Difference

Five core programmes, one shared purpose: every child deserves a dignified life.

Programme 1

Holiday Outreach

During Christmas and festive seasons, we distribute food parcels, clothing, gifts, and essential household items to hundreds of vulnerable families across Ondo City. Our Christmas Eve Community Feast brings joy to children who might otherwise go without celebration.

Programme 2

New Mother Support

Vulnerable new mothers receive cash assistance, baby clothing, nappies, and essential items. We believe every newborn deserves the best possible start, regardless of their family's circumstances.

Programme 3

Food Security

Regular feeding programmes deliver nutritious meals directly to children in underserved communities. Good nutrition is the foundation of learning, growth, and healthy development — we make sure no child is left hungry.

Programme 4

Education Support

School bags, books, uniforms, and stationery are provided to children who would otherwise be unable to attend school. Education is the most powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty — and we help unlock it.
